Manage a passport.

The delete token is the only credential this site has, so it is also the only thing that can answer whether your passport is still up, how many people opened it, and when it comes down. There are no accounts, and adding one to show you a number would be a bad trade.

Paste the whole link. The part that matters is taken out of it.

If you no longer have it, nothing on this page can be done and the passport comes down by itself when it expires. It was never stored in a form anyone can read back, including us.

This form accepts 20 attempts an hour from one network, right or wrong, counted in fixed hours rather than a sliding one. Past that it stops answering for the rest of the hour and then starts again on its own. Nothing about your passport or your token changes while it is waiting.

Neither value is sent anywhere except to this site, and neither is written into the address bar. If you came here from a link that had both in it, they were in the part after the # that browsers never send to a server, and that part is cleared as soon as this page reads it.

Send

Email the link to someone.

Used for this message, and named in your own copy if you ask for one, and not stored. A one-way hash of it counts that day's messages, so that nobody can flood one inbox through us; it stops counting at midnight UTC and a later cleanup deletes it. There is no list, no follow-up and nothing for them to unsubscribe from.

Shown in the message, beside the agent's name, under a line saying we did not write either of them. Leave it blank and it says "Someone". It is not in the subject: the subject is the same fixed line on every message we send, because a subject line is the most convincing part of an email and none of it is a stranger's to write.

Sending email is not switched on for this deployment. Copy the link above and send it however you normally would; nothing about the passport depends on it.

Gallery

List it, or take it off the list.

One pick, and it is the only thing the gallery sorts and filters by. It is public, and the owner can change it later only by unlisting and listing again.

Open the gallery

Unlisting puts it back on the thirty-day clock from that moment, and deletes the votes it collected. A passport listed a second time starts from zero, which is honest about what those votes were for.

Keep or withdraw

Renew it, or take it down.

Renewing moves the date rather than adding to it: a passport renewed on its last day and one renewed on its first both get the same thirty days from now. You can renew as often as you like, and nothing renews by itself, which is the point.

Deletion is immediate and irreversible. There is no undo and no archive. Afterwards the link behaves exactly like a link that never existed, which is deliberate: a visitor must not be able to tell a deleted passport from an invented one. Anyone who already read it still has what they read, which no delete button anywhere can change.

Deleted
